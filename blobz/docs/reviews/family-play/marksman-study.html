Two goo stand in one lane. Five stand apart for Volley. Choose which Marksman card helps each shot.

Pick a card, then try both shot types.

Family test

  1. With labels covered, ask which card they want when enemies stand behind each other.
  2. Fire both shot types with each card. Ask whether extra Volley targets meant extra hits on one boss.
  3. Swap card positions, play the icon frames once and repeat.
Study rules

Pierce lets a basic bolt continue through one additional enemy. Two more targets raises Volley from three to five separate targets. It does not add repeated Volley hits to one enemy. Damage, cooldowns and enemy movement are omitted.
