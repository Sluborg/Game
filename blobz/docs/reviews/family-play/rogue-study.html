Mechanics and study simplifications

One Bigger whirl card raises the 72 world-pixel radius to 93.6. One Harder push card raises knockback from 200 to 300 and also applies to basic hits in the game. Each whirl hit still does 22 damage and stuns for 0.6 seconds. The game's whirl checks distance to the edge of each enemy.

The room holds starting positions fixed between casts. The arrows and travel distances illustrate relative knockback. Enemy AI, damage, cooldowns and collision physics are outside this study.
