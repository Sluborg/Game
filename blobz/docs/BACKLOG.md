## Dungeons (combat adventure) - Stefan 2026-11-14
- The team goes down together into a dungeon (entrance on the map), more combat oriented than the meadow.
- Classes to pick (per player, in the lobby or at the dungeon door): Präst (heals, shields), Bågskytt (ranged),
  Krigare (melee, knockback), Riddare (tank, protects friends). Art: class gear as blob hats/held items.
- Level up inside a dungeon run: simple "pick 1 of 3" cards (skill or effect), kid-readable icons.
- Loot/rewards: either everyone gets something, or roll/pass so kids learn to share: the screen shows
  what you have vs the new item, and you should pass if you do not need it. Open question (board).
- Tiny Dungeon (Kenney, already vendored) fits the look; goo enemies already drawn.

## Night Temple / night mode - Stefan 2026-11-14
- A temple somewhere on the map. If all blobz go there they can do a ritual that starts night mode:
  monsters attack now and then, the team defends the base.
- Open question: combat in the base mode at all, or only in dungeons? (board). Milestone 50 (goo waves)
  is on hold until this is decided.
