# Art prompts (ChatGPT / image models)

Style anchor for every prompt: **Kenney "Tiny" packs** (Tiny Town, Tiny Dungeon, Tiny Farm):
16x16 pixel art, 1px dark plum outline (#3f2631), flat colors with one highlight shade,
no anti-aliasing, no gradients, top-down 3/4 view, bright cheerful palette.

## 10. Title / key art (1920x1080, landscape)
> Pixel art title screen in the exact style of Kenney's Tiny Town and Tiny Dungeon asset packs:
> 16x16 tile grid upscaled 4x with nearest-neighbor, 1px dark plum outlines, flat colors.
> A sunny meadow seen from a 3/4 top-down view with a small red-roofed cottage base, two blue flags,
> round green and orange autumn trees, red-capped mushrooms, logs and gold coins scattered.
> Five cute slime blobs (sky blue, pink, green, yellow, purple) hover-glide across the grass with
> small speed puffs behind them; one wears a tiny gold crown, one a red cap, one a party hat,
> one a white flower. Two blobs push a wooden treasure chest together toward the cottage.
> Leave the top third calm (sky-green grass) for the logo. No text, no UI, no watermark.

## 20. Logo (transparent PNG, 1024x512)
> Pixel art game logo reading "BLOBZ" in chunky rounded 16px-grid letters, white fill with a 2px
> dark plum outline and a soft blue drop shadow, Kenney Tiny pack style, a small sky-blue slime
> blob peeking over the letter Z. Transparent background, no other text.

## 30. App icon (1024x1024)
> Pixel art app icon, Kenney Tiny Dungeon style: one sky-blue slime blob with a tiny gold crown,
> centered on a grass tile background, 1px dark plum outline, flat colors, rounded-square safe area.
